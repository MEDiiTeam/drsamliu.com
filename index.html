About

A doctor who listens first

Good medicine starts with time and attention. My consultations are never rushed: we talk through your concerns properly, I examine and investigate where needed, and you leave with a clear explanation and a clear plan.

I studied medicine at University College London and spent more than ten years training at London's teaching hospitals, specialising in cardiology at Barts Heart Centre, one of Europe's largest cardiothoracic centres. I hold consultant posts in both Cardiology and General Internal Medicine, so I can look after the whole patient: the heart, and the conditions that so often travel with it.

Alongside my NHS consultant work, I am a founding member and Medical Director of MEDii, a doctor-led private medical centre in central London, where I lead the clinical team and care for many international and Chinese-speaking patients.

Services

How I can help

Cardiology consultations

Chest pain, palpitations, breathlessness, dizziness or fainting, high blood pressure and cholesterol: assessed carefully and explained clearly.

Heart tests, on site

ECG, echocardiogram, Holter rhythm monitoring and 24-hour blood pressure monitoring at MEDii, often on the same day, with results I review and explain myself.

General internal medicine

Care beyond the heart. As a consultant in both specialties I assess unexplained symptoms and coexisting conditions as a whole, not in silos.

Bilingual & international care

Care in English or Chinese, with reports and letters available in both, and help coordinating care between the UK and overseas.

Beyond the clinic

Improving how healthcare works

As a founding member and Medical Director of MEDii, I work on how private healthcare is designed: shorter waits, clearer answers, and technology that gives doctors more time with their patients. I speak on these themes, including AI in healthcare at the 3rd UK–China Private Healthcare Summit, and welcome conversations with clinicians, partners and organisations who share that aim.
